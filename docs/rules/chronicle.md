# Chronicle, campaign persistence, and eras

## Persistence model

- A game has a winner; its post-game **Chronicle Phase** transforms the stored world for the next game.
- A **campaign** has no inherent endpoint.
- An **era** is a group-defined scoring window across games. Suggested end is 20 lineage points. At era end, highest score wins; after the current Chronicle completes, scores reset and all other state persists (CR pp. 5, 37).
- Players need not remain constant. The group should pre-agree how new/returning players receive lineages and points (CR p. 5).

## Durable state to preserve

- Chancellor/Citizen/Exile roles and color ownership.
- Active/dormant legacy faces and each player's starting adviser.
- Current Oathkeeper goal.
- Six foundation faces, including whether an active legacy locks an altered face.
- Atlas ordering: Recent vs Forgotten site stacks, Empire divider, and cards stored on each site.
- World deck/discard-derived composition, Dispossessed denizens, suited reserves, edifice deck order, Vision set.
- Reliquary pile (max 5 after Chronicle), Grand Scepter ownership, and player-held/stored cards.
- Lineage-point scorepad, current era endpoint, and Chronicle narrative/constellation if used (CR pp. 36-41).

## Ordered Chronicle tasks

Resolve the five task cards in order (CR p. 36).

### 1. The Sun

1. Reveal all facedown player relics.
2. Activate dormant legacies whose goals are met; discard dormant failures; alter foundations for newly active legacies.
3. Exiles (not Imperials) make active unmet legacies dormant. This does not restore foundations.
4. Enforce maximum three active legacies in turn order, then shuffle legacy deck.
5. Determine goals with special tie rules: Imperial-only ties are friendly; any Exile tie makes the goal unmet; bandits do not compete. If Grand Scepter is absent, winner meets that legacy goal.
6. Crown winner Chancellor. If an Exile won, break the old Empire, reactivate their newly dormant legacies, convert all former Imperials (including stored players) to Exiles, clear Reliquary, remove Imperial site warbands, and place one Imperial warband on each winner-ruled site. If a Citizen won, former Chancellor becomes Citizen.
7. Assign Throne to winner, World to People's Favor holder, Beacon to Darkest Secret holder. Winner assigns an unheld-banner task; one person may not retain both World and Beacon (CR p. 36).

### 2. The Throne

1. Offer Citizenship to Exiles; accepting activates newly dormant legacies and converts each site they ruled to one Imperial warband. Special restoration applies if former Chancellor accepts.
2. Shape Empire: Chancellor may remove their site warbands but must retain/establish at least one ruled site; discard non-ruled denizens except intact edifices and Enduring-site cards. Discarded ruined edifices go beneath edifice deck.
3. Build one edifice optionally: expose top intact edifice per suit; bury a denizen at a ruled site to place the matching edifice; reshuffle unused candidates into edifice deck.
4. Stock Reliquary with Throne holder's relics except Grand Scepter; trim pile to 5.
5. Scatter all other discarded/player relics into empty site slots Hinterward, remainder to relic deck.
6. Score winner 3 plus legacy bonus points from matching cards at ruled sites (not advisers). Winner may convert 2 of their scored points into 1 point for another player, repeatedly. Check era end.
7. Set Oathkeeper goal to winning Vision if Vision victory; otherwise choose a different goal (CR p. 37).

### 3. The World

1. Separate all five Visions.
2. Shuffle regional discards and player advisers; return all but nine denizens to world deck.
3. Make three stacks of three, two faceup and one facedown.
4. Choose a faceup stack to protect: secretly retain one as next-game starting adviser; add its other two to world deck.
5. Put the remaining six denizens behind Dispossessed (CR p. 38).

### 4. The Beacon

1. Choose three different suits and draw 3/2/1 newcomers respectively from suited reserves (excluding Dispossessed/Edifices). If a chosen reserve is short, return older Dispossessed cards to suited reserves, except the six just added by World.
2. Keep one newcomer facedown as next-game starting adviser; add the rest to world deck.
3. Optionally restore one altered foundation to normal unless an active legacy currently alters it (CR p. 39).

### 5. The Stars

1. Winner optionally records/names a constellation and group narrative.
2. Archive sites not ruled by winner: remove pieces, keep relics/intact edifices on site, stack Hinterward with Cradlemost on top, put Empire divider on top, and insert on Recent side above older Atlas sites.
3. Return pieces to banks. Consolidate winner-ruled sites Cradleward with their cards, leaving only Hinterward empty slots.
4. Either continue or store: Empire sites/cards above divider in Atlas; player pieces/advisers/legacies preserving face; Chancellor/Grand Scepter/Reliquary separately; Visions and world denizens behind World divider; remaining pieces boxed (CR pp. 40-41).
