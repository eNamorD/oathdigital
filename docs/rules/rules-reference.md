# Rules reference

## Game model and roles

*Oath* is a continuing-history game. A game changes later games; a campaign has no intrinsic end. A bounded competitive sequence is an **era**, whose endpoint is chosen by the group (suggested default: 20 lineage points). Players may join or leave between games (CR p. 5).

Each player controls a lineage/color and one pawn. Roles are:

- **Exile**: independent player; may win as Usurper or Visionary.
- **Chancellor**: head of the Empire and holder of the Grand Scepter.
- **Citizen**: Imperial player allied structurally with the Chancellor but able to raid Imperials, attack Imperial sites, and win as Successor.
- **Imperial**: collective term for Chancellor and Citizens (CR pp. 34-35).

## Core state and terminology

- The map has three regions in order: **Cradle**, **Provinces**, **Hinterland**. **Hinterward** is top-to-bottom Cradle, then Provinces, then Hinterland; **Cradleward** reverses that order (CR pp. 9, 45).
- A pawn's **site** is its current location. A player's board holds favor, secrets, warbands, and Supply state (CR p. 14).
- Six denizen suits exist: Discord, Arcane, Order, Hearth, Beast, and Nomad. A site may not exceed its printed denizen capacity; an adviser area normally holds at most three cards (CR p. 14).
- A site is ruled by exactly one player or by the bandits. Any player warband there establishes rule. After any action, an empty site is immediately filled with bandit warbands equal to capacity (CR p. 15).
- A player has access to cards at their pawn's site, sites they rule, and their play area (relics, banners, advisers, legacies). Power costs can be placed only on empty cards (CR p. 15).
- Relics and banners have no holding limit and can be stolen. Facedown relics have no power, but still count for victory and legacy conditions (CR pp. 15, 25, 27; NF p. 14).

## Setup

### Very first game only

1. Prepare common pieces; start banners on Mob and Wandering Flame with one relevant resource; use Oath of Supremacy; shuffle relics (CR p. 6).
2. Deal eight faceup sites from Recent Sites. Populate starting resources, facedown relics equal to slots, bandits equal to capacity, and a matching ruined edifice at each Homeland (CR p. 6).
3. Build a 60-denizen pool (10 of each suit), seed regional discards and player hands, and build the world deck with Vision packets: 10 denizens + 2 Visions above 15 denizens + 3 Visions above the remaining denizens (CR p. 6).
4. Put 3 favor in each suit bank, +1 per bank at 5+ players, and +1 to a bank per matching edifice on the map. Initialize Round, Usurper Limiter (round 4), Visions Drawn, and relic deck (CR p. 7).
5. Randomize seating/first player. Everyone is an Exile with 1 favor, 1 secret, 3 warbands on board, full-left Supply, and 2 random legacies. In order, place pawn, keep one legacy dormant, keep one of three denizens as adviser, and discard the rest to the next regional discard cycle (CR p. 7).

No one begins Chancellor or Oathkeeper; the first player to satisfy Supremacy takes the title (NF p. 8). Do not reuse first-game setup for a new era unless fully resetting the box (CR p. 7; NF p. 9).

### Later games

1. Initialize map tracks, end die if Imperials play, relic deck, prior Oathkeeper goal/title, and stored world-deck denizens with Visions removed (CR p. 8).
2. Players recover their stored color, legacies, starting adviser, 1 favor, 1 secret, 3 board warbands, and Supply. Imperials use purple warbands. Chancellor takes the Chancellor board, Grand Scepter, and one chosen facedown Reliquary relic (CR p. 8).
3. If a Chancellor exists, restore Imperial sites from Recent Sites until the Empire divider, intact edifices/faceup denizens, stored facedown relics, and one Imperial warband each (CR p. 9).
4. With no Chancellor: force Foundation I to Wide Horizons and V to Mob's Favor; restore sites with ruined edifices, stored relics, capacity bandits; clear Reliquary; fill empty relic slots; turn stored Citizens into Exiles; choose first player; use Usurper Limiter and no end die (CR p. 9).
5. Resolve six foundation setup cards in order. Their normal/altered faces determine map filling, banks/resources, player wealth, world composition, and banner side (CR pp. 10-11).
6. Populate site resources, then build the world deck from the foundation-determined starting denizen stack, combining Vision packets as instructed (CR p. 12).
7. In order, players place pawn (subject to Imperial placement), choose adviser, and reveal cards as allowed (CR p. 13).

## Round and turn flow

A round gives each player one turn, clockwise from Chancellor; without a Chancellor, start from the first-player marker. Each turn resolves **Wake -> Act -> Rest** (CR p. 17).

### Wake

1. If an Exile holds Oathkeeper, flip it to Usurper unless limited; if already Usurper, they win.
2. If an Exile has a revealed Vision and currently meets it, they win.
3. Use any Wake powers once each.
4. Built-in **Take Wealth**: take one favor or secret from the pawn's site if no enemy pawn is present (CR p. 17).

### Act

Take actions in any order, repeated or omitted. Major actions cost Supply; minor actions cost 0 by default (CR p. 17).

### Rest

1. Use each desired Rest power once.
2. Return favor from denizens to matching suit banks. Return secrets from denizens/relics to the player's board. Flip the player's facedown secrets faceup.
3. Refresh Supply to the space corresponding to warbands remaining in the off-board warband bank, then one extra step left per Supply left unspent this turn (CR p. 18).

## Major actions

### Search - 2 to 4 Supply

Choose world deck or current-region discard. Regional discard costs 2; world cost is the Visions Drawn track value. Draw one at a time, normally three; stop on a Vision drawn from world and advance the track. Keep one and discard the rest to the next-region pile (Cradle -> Provinces -> Hinterland -> Cradle). Play the kept card to the site or advisers subject to icons/capacity, or discard it. Playing a denizen to a site gains one matching-suit favor (CR p. 20).

### Travel - 1 to 4 Supply before modifiers

Choose a destination and pay the region matrix:

| From | Cradle | Provinces | Hinterland |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cradle | 1 (other Cradle) | 2 | 4 |
| Provinces | 2 | 2 | 2 |
| Hinterland | 4 | 2 | 3 (other Hinterland) |

Then move the pawn. Site powers such as Coast, Island, Mountain, and Pass modify or restrict this (CR p. 21).

### Campaign - 2 Supply

Choose exactly one type (CR pp. 22-23):

- **Conquest**: defender is the ruler of the attacker's site. Must target that site; may add other sites ruled by the same defender.
- **Raid**: defender is an enemy pawn at the attacker's site. Must target the pawn; may also target any number (including zero) of that player's faceup relics and banners.

Sequence: choose targets; form attacker dice up to board warband force and defender dice from targets (raid pawn adds 2); attacker then defender use battle plans; attacker rolls and counts swords while suffering skull losses; attacker may sacrifice force warbands for +1 attack each; defender rolls shields/doublers and adds target-force warbands; attacker wins only if attack strictly exceeds defense; defeated player loses half its force rounded down and returns the rest to board (bandits lose all targeted forces); resolve conquest placement or raid theft/discards/burn/move-pawn; resolve remaining end/victory/defeat battle plans.

On successful raid: take targeted faceup relics/banners; discard defender's facedown advisers and facedown relics (removed until Chronicle), burn half defender favor rounded down, and relocate defender pawn to another site (CR p. 23).

### Muster - 1 Supply

Place one favor on an empty denizen at the pawn's site. Gain one warband to board plus one for each faceup adviser matching that denizen's suit (CR p. 24).

### Trade - 1 Supply

Use an empty denizen at the pawn's site. For favor, place one secret and gain favor based on matching faceup advisers. For secrets, place one favor and burn one favor, then gain secrets based on matching faceup advisers. Exact yield is printed on the player board (CR p. 24; NF p. 13).

### Forge - 1 Supply

Requires ruling the pawn's site, a Forge icon, and three empty denizens. Place the site's printed favor/secrets onto those three denizens, one resource per denizen in any order, then draw a facedown relic (CR p. 25).

### Recover - 1+ Supply

Roll two defense dice; repeatedly pay 1 Supply for two more dice. If accumulated shields, including doublers, meet/exceed the site's Recover Difficulty, peek at site relics and take one facedown (CR p. 25).

### Challenge - 1 Supply

Choose a banner not held by the actor. If enemy-held, pawns must share a site. Challenger must have more of the relevant resource than is on the banner. Resolve the banner's left-ribbon removal/distribution, take it, then place more relevant resources on it than it held before the challenge (CR p. 26).

## Minor actions

Default cost is 0 Supply. Core minor actions include:

- play/discard a facedown adviser as if just searched;
- use an Action power;
- reveal/peek at relics held by the player;
- move warbands between board and a ruled site at the pawn;
- negotiate with a co-located player, exchanging favor and relics (not secrets/banners), with mutual consent;
- banner-specific resource placement;
- self-exile under its cost/conditions;
- applicable Imperial warband transfers (CR p. 27; CR pp. 32, 34-35).

## Victory and game end

### Oathkeeper and Usurper

The current Oathkeeper goal is one of: Supremacy (rule more sites than every other player, at least one), Protection (hold most relics, at least one), The People (People's Favor), or Devotion (Darkest Secret). Title changes immediately after an action changes qualification; current holder retains ties, otherwise holder chooses among tied qualifiers. Bandits do not compete (CR p. 16).

An Exile who starts a turn holding Oathkeeper flips it to Usurper; if they start their next turn still Usurper, they win. With no Empire, flipping is barred until round 4 (CR pp. 16, 19).

### Visions

An Exile may reveal one true Vision. At the start of their turn they win if its goal is met and at least three Visions have been drawn from the world deck. The four true Visions mirror the four Oathkeeper goals. Conspiracy is not a victory Vision (CR pp. 16, 30).

### Empire endings

At ends of rounds 5/6/7, if Chancellor or Citizen is Oathkeeper, roll the end die; Chancellor wins on 6, then 5-6, then 3-6 respectively. At round 8 resolve War Exhaustion: qualifying Imperial Oathkeeper -> Chancellor; Exile Usurper; qualifying revealed Vision (tie order Conquest, Rebellion, Sanctuary, Faith); otherwise Chancellor (CR p. 19).

If Chancellor would win, a Citizen satisfying the current Successor goal wins instead. Successor mappings are: Supremacy -> more relics than Chancellor and every other Citizen; The People -> Darkest Secret; Protection -> People's Favor; Devotion -> Grand Scepter (CR p. 19).

Without Empire, round 8 uses War Exhaustion while skipping Stable Regime and Chancellor steps; if still no winner, Oathkeeper wins, otherwise random (CR p. 19).

## Rules precedence and timing

- Card powers may add actions and trigger effects while using glossary-defined terms (CR p. 42).
- "Cannot" overrides "can"; "must" is mandatory; "may" is optional. If multiple "cannot" rules conflict, use the more specific one (CR p. 42).
- Component supply is normally limited; dice are unlimited. If a limited component runs out, ignore additional placement (CR p. 42).
- Public and private information are enumerated on CR p. 42. A player may choose/share all their private information.
- Consent must be actively enforced when an "Enemies cannot" persistent effect applies; forgetting waives it for that moment (CR p. 43).
